Your return policy is one of the most important pages on your Shopify store. A good policy builds trust and increases sales. A bad one costs you money and customers.

**67% of shoppers check the return policy before making a purchase.** And 30% won't buy from a store with a strict return policy — no matter how good the products are.

## The Balancing Act

A great return policy balances two things:
- **Generous enough** to build trust and convert sales
- **Strict enough** to prevent abuse and protect margins

## 5 Best Practices

### 1. Set a Clear Time Window

The standard is **30 days** from delivery. Data shows:

| Window | Trust Impact | Abuse Risk |
|---|---|---|
| 14 days | Low trust | Low abuse |
| 30 days | High trust | Medium abuse |
| 60 days | Very high trust | High abuse |
| 90 days | Maximum trust | Very high abuse |

**Recommendation:** 30 days with a 14-day "quick refund" option for items under $50.

### 2. Use Tiered Policies

Not all products should have the same policy:

| Product Type | Window | Fee | Auto-approve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apparel | 30 days | 0% | Under $200 |
| Electronics | 14 days | 15% restocking | Under $100 |
| Clearance | Final sale | N/A | Never |
| Gift cards | Non-returnable | N/A | N/A |

### 3. Automate Approvals for Low-Risk Returns

Studies show that **85% of returns under $50 are legitimate**. Auto-approving these saves time while keeping risk low.

In Shopigent Returns, create a policy:
```
Name: Quick refund (under $50)
Max Days: 30
Max Amount: $50
Auto-approve: ✅
```

### 4. Charge Restocking Fees Strategically

Restocking fees are controversial but effective:

| Fee | When to Use | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | Standard items, under $200 | Best customer experience |
| 10-15% | High-value items, electronics | Reduces frivolous returns |
| 25% | Special order items | Covers handling costs |

**Pro tip:** Waive the fee for store credit instead of cash refunds — customers spend 30% more with store credit.

### 5. Track and Analyze Return Reasons

Data from your returns tells you what's wrong with your products or listings:

| Return Reason | What It Means | What to Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Too small" | Sizing guide unclear | Add size chart, measurements |
| "Different from photo" | Product images misleading | Better photography, 360° views |
| "Poor quality" | QC issues | Review supplier/manufacturer |
| "Arrived damaged" | Packing/shipping issue | Better packaging, new carrier |

## Sample Return Policy Template

```
**Return Policy**

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in original packaging.

**Quick refund items:** Orders under $200 are auto-approved.
**High-value items:** Orders over $200 may be subject to a 15%
restocking fee.

**How to return:**
1. Visit our return portal: [link]
2. Enter your email and order number
3. Select items to return
4. Print the prepaid label
5. Drop off at any post office

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive
the item.
```
